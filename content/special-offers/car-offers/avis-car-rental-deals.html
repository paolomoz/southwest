Book a car

Earn up to 3,000 Rapid Rewards Points®.

Plus, save up to 35% on base rates.

Now through January 31, 2027, you can earn up to 3,000 points and save up to 35% on base rates on qualifying Avis rentals. This offer is valid on all car groups on rentals in the US and Canada.

  • Rent for one to two days, and earn 600 points
  • Rent for three to six days, and earn 1,800 points
  • Rent for seven days or longer and earn 3,000 points

For reservations, book below. The Coupon Code and Avis Worldwide Discount (AWD) fields will be entered for you. Simply remember to provide your Rapid Rewards account number when completing your reservation or at rental check in to receive your points.

You may also book on the Car tab online at Southwest.com. When booking please be sure to use the coupon code and Avis Discount Code listed on this page and provide your Rapid Rewards account number.
