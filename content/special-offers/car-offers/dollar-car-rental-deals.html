Save up to 15% on the base rate*

+ earn 1,200 Rapid Rewards® points.

Rent a car with Dollar and save up to 15%* on the base rate, plus earn up to 1,200 Rapid Rewards points. Valid at participating Dollar airport locations.

  • Rent for 1 - 2 days: Earn 600 points.
  • Rent for 3 or more days: Earn 1,200 points.

To take advantage of this offer, book a qualifying reservation through the booking widget on this page. The CD code and Promotion Code will already be entered for you. Simply remember to provide your Rapid Rewards account number at time of booking or at check-in to receive your points.

You may also receive this offer by booking online through the Car tab at Southwest.com®. When booking, please enter 3055251 in the Corporate Discount field and 101611 in the Promotion Code field, and be sure to provide your Rapid Rewards account number at time of booking or at check-in.
