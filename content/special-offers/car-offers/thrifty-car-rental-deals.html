Rapid Rewards® Promotions

Discover 20+ ways to earn points toward your next trip or more.

Book a car

Earn up to 1,200 Rapid Rewards® points.

and save 10% on base rates*with Thrifty.

Now through December 31, 2026, you can earn up to 1,200 points and save 10% on base rates* on the rental of a mid-size or larger vehicle with Thrifty. Taxes, fees, and options excluded.

  • Rent for 1 - 2 days: Earn 600 points.
  • Rent for 3 - 6 days: Earn 1,200 points.

To take advantage of this offer, book a qualifying reservation through the booking widget on this page. The Promotion Code field will already be entered for you. Simply remember to provide your Rapid Rewards account number at time of booking or at check-in to receive your points.

Terms and Conditions:

*Base rate includes time and mileage charges only. Taxes, tax reimbursement, age differential charges, fees, surcharges and optional service charges, such as refueling, are not included. Advance reservation, Rapid Rewards® account number and Promotion Code "102356" for rentals of one day or longer are required. Promotion Code must be provided at time of reservation or offer is void. Rentals of 1-2 days earn 600 points, 3+ days earn 1,200 points. Valid on mid-size or larger vehicles. Offer not valid and rate change or additional fees may apply in the event of reservation modification, early / late vehicle return or if not returning vehicle to renting location. Offer has no cash value, and may not be used with other discounts, promotions or special rates. Age, driver, credit, and qualifying rate restrictions for the renting location apply. Frequent Flyer Surcharge of $1.50 per day, up to a maximum of $8.00 per rental, may apply. Discounts in local currency upon redemption. Subject to availability, blackout periods and additional restrictions. Offer valid for vehicle pick-up on or before December 31, 2026. Any points earned over 600 per rental are considered bonus points. Bonus points do not count toward A-List, A-List Preferred, or Companion Pass® qualification. Please allow up to eight weeks for points to post to your account. All Rapid Rewards rules and regulations apply and can be found at Southwest.com/rrterms.
