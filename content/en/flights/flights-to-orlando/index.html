Flights to Orlando – Orlando International Airport (MCO)

Ready for some sunshine? A flight to Orlando International Airport (MCO) is your ticket to a trip filled with world-class theme parks and family-friendly fun. Flying with Southwest to MCO puts you right in the center of the action, so it's easy to kickstart your magical vacation or warm-weather escape.

Orlando International Airport (MCO) vs Orlando Sanford International Airport (SFB)

Two airports serve travelers who fly to Orlando: Orlando International Airport (MCO) and Orlando Sanford International Airport (SFB). If you're having the "MCO vs SFB" debate, consider this: For many travelers, it all comes down to convenience. Orlando International Airport (MCO) is much closer to Orlando's main attractions, while SFB is located approximately 25 miles north of the city. Choosing MCO means less travel time and more vacation time, so it's the best bet for most travelers.

Getting From the Airport to Orlando's Theme Parks and Attractions

Rideshares, taxis, and rental cars are all quickly accessible after landing at the Southwest terminal at MCO. If you're staying at a resort, check whether it offers an airport shuttle, or book with a private Orlando airport shuttle service. Because Southwest MCO arrivals put you so close to the resort areas, your journey from baggage claim to the front gates is short, practical, and low-stress, so you can get the fun started sooner.

What's the Best Time to Visit Orlando?

Your family's schedule and tolerance for crowds are big factors to consider when you're deciding on the best time to visit Orlando. Remember that Orlando is a popular destination, especially during school holidays, so summer and winter breaks often mean larger crowds and higher flight demand. If comfortable weather and shorter lines are your top priority, you might want to visit during the “shoulder seasons.” Spring and fall offer pleasant temperatures perfect for exploring the parks or enjoying Florida's natural beauty. No matter when you choose to travel (but especially in the summer), it's always a good idea to pack light layers and a poncho for those quick afternoon showers.
